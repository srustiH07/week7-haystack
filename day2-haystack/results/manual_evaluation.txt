# Overall Comparison

BM25 performed well when the question contained keywords that directly
matched the document content.

Dense Retrieval performed better for questions requiring semantic
similarity, especially when the wording of the question differed from
the wording used in the documents.

Overall, Dense Retrieval showed better semantic retrieval performance,
while BM25 remained effective for exact keyword-based searches.
